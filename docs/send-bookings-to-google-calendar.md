**Google Calendar** adds confirmed appointments, hires and reservations to one Google Calendar. It moves or removes them if they change or are cancelled.

It only sends. Changes made in Google Calendar do not come back to the site, so the site stays the source of truth.

## Set it up

1. Open **Google Calendar Settings**.
2. Paste the **Service account key (JSON)** from your Google Cloud account.
3. Share your calendar with the service account address shown on the page.
4. Enter the **Calendar ID**.
5. Press **Save Settings**.

Use **Send test event** to check it works. It saves nothing and uses the key and calendar already saved.

The Google Cloud side of this is fiddly, so ask your developer if you get stuck.
